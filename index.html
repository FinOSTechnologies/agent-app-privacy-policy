1. About this policy

Authorised field agents use it to onboard and manage customers, create and service loan and savings accounts, and record repayments and deposits. This policy covers information handled through the App and connected FinOS services.

3. Information handled

Agent account information

The App handles agent names, staff or employee identifiers, usernames, phone numbers, email addresses, office or branch and role details, passwords, and authentication tokens.

Customer information

Agents enter customer names, dates of birth, gender, phone numbers, customer identifiers and addresses. Customer records can include identity and KYC information, photographs, documents, signatures, family or nominee details, and other onboarding or servicing information.

Financial information

The App handles loan and savings account details, account status, balances or amounts, repayment schedules, transactions, charges, deposits, collections, receipts, related notes and service records.

Device and connection information

When the App connects to a FinOS service, that service receives the device’s IP address and request information. The App uses device model, operating system, app version and technical identifiers where needed for app functions and security.

Device location

The App does not access or collect the agent’s device location. It has no location permissions or device-location tracking service. The client map screen displays client coordinate records received from the configured FinOS service; it does not read the agent’s GPS location.

Photos, files and diagnostics

When an agent chooses to take or select a customer image, the App uses the camera or selected device media. Agents can select documents to upload and export or share report files. Camera and media are accessed in response to these actions. The App creates technical request and diagnostic logs to support operation; it does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring or Cloud Messaging.

4. How information is used

Information comes directly from agents, from customers through agents, and from the connected FinOS service. The connected service receives IP address and request data when the App connects.

FinOS uses information to:

  • provide and operate the App and financial services;
  • authenticate agents and manage access;
  • onboard and serve customers and manage their accounts;
  • record and synchronise loans, savings, repayments, deposits and collections;
  • meet applicable KYC, anti-money-laundering, audit and regulatory requirements;
  • protect services, investigate fraud or security issues, and provide support; and
  • maintain and improve the App and services.

Where consent is the legal basis, the party responsible for the customer relationship (FinOS or the participating institution) gives a clear notice describing the personal data, purposes, services enabled, how to withdraw consent, how to exercise rights and how to complain to the Data Protection Board of India. FinOS processes information without consent only where applicable law permits it.

5. User responsibilities

Agents must use the App only for authorised work, follow their participating institution’s instructions, and enter accurate information collected for the relevant service. Agents must protect their login credentials and devices, sign out when appropriate, and promptly report a lost device or suspected unauthorised access to their institution and FinOS. Agents must give customers the notices required by their institution and applicable law and must not enter information they are not authorised to handle.

7. Sharing

FinOS does not use personal data for advertising or sell it. FinOS shares information as needed to provide services, meet legal duties and protect people and services.

  • FinOS services and participating institutions: The App sends account, customer, financial, document, photograph and synchronisation information to the configured FinOS service for use by the participating institution. Customer record requests may also need to go to the relevant bank or financial institution.
  • Institution personnel and agents: Authorised bank personnel and agents access records according to their assigned roles and permissions at the participating institution.
  • Google Maps: The App uses Google Maps to render client map screens. The map camera is centered on client coordinates returned by the configured FinOS service, so Google Maps processes those coordinates along with map requests and associated network information. These are client records; the App does not read the agent’s device location.
  • Service providers: FinOS service providers host, secure, maintain or support services and process information for those purposes.
  • Authorities: FinOS discloses information when required by law, legal process or a regulator, or when needed to protect people, rights or service security.
  • Business changes: Information transfers as part of a merger, reorganisation, financing or sale of business assets are handled under applicable law.

8. Storage, security and retention

The App stores information on the device for sign-in and offline work. Authentication state is encrypted using Android Keystore. Other locally cached app records use Android app-private database storage and are not encrypted at the application level. FinOS restricts access to personal data to authorised people and service providers who need it for their work.

The App accepts only HTTPS server addresses. Its Android network security configuration blocks cleartext HTTP for all domains. The App uses HTTPS/TLS to send information to FinOS services.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. FinOS notifies affected parties and authorities of a personal data breach as required by applicable law.

9. Offline use and synchronisation

The App stores information on the device for offline work and later synchronisation. Records remain on the device until the relevant App feature synchronises them with the configured FinOS service. Anyone who can unlock the device or access the agent’s session may see offline records, so agents must protect their devices. Uninstalling the App, signing out or deactivating an agent account does not delete records already sent to the FinOS service or the institution’s backend; those records follow the applicable retention rules.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you can ask FinOS to provide access to your personal data and information about its use or sharing, correct inaccurate information, delete information that is no longer needed, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and address a privacy grievance. Customer record requests may also need to be sent to the relevant bank or financial institution.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 provides for a person to nominate someone to exercise their rights in the event of death or incapacity, and to complain to the Data Protection Board of India after using the grievance process. These rights take effect under the Act’s notified commencement schedule.

Send requests to support@finos.tech. Include enough information for FinOS to verify your identity and find the relevant record. Do not email credentials or identity documents. FinOS responds within the period required by applicable law.
